## Videos

Video files are stored in `public/videos/`; the `/sources` route lists each source and creator. The Commons clips were downloaded from their linked originals and transcoded to H.264 MP4 for browser playback. The Madhubani clip is a 42-second excerpt (04:02–04:44) from Bharti Dayal's film; that edit and transcode are shared under CC BY-SA 3.0. The Indus clip shows the Great Bath at Mohenjo-daro as site context, not the Dancing Girl statuette. The Ajanta page uses the broader Indian-painting history film, which includes Ajanta. The Mughal page uses a motion study made for this exhibit from the public-domain Akbarnama image credited above; no unrelated video is presented as footage of that manuscript.
